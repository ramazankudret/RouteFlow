calibration drift

Every view puts prediction and measurement side by side and never averages the two error kinds together. The scatters show individual traces, the spread shows the shape of the bias, the per-node view shows where cold starts are expensive, and the drift view shows the estimator losing ground as the window fills. Warm keeps the t_queue blue and cold the t_load orange used everywhere else in the console, so colour means the same thing on every screen.
